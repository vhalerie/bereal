BeReal — Let's BeReal Interest Circles
Prototype link: https://vhalerie.github.io/bereal/bereal_prototype.html
Prototype Instructions
==============================

GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. The phone frame loads on the My Friends tab by default.
2. Use the ↺ Reset interests button (top right, outside the phone) to restart the prototype at any time.


COLD START FLOW
---------------
3. Tap the "Let's BeReal" tab in the top navigation.
4. The Interest Picker screen appears. Your location is shown as Paris, France.
5. Tap any interest card to select it (e.g. K-pop, Running, Photography). Selected cards show a white checkmark.
6. Tap a selected card again to deselect it.
7. Select at least 1 interest to activate the "Let's go" button (it turns white when ready).
8. Tap "Let's go" to enter the Let's BeReal feed.


LBR FEED
---------
9. You land on the Let's BeReal feed, showing BeReals from your selected interest circles.
10. Filter pills appear at the top — "All" is selected by default.
11. Tap any circle pill (e.g. 🎵 Kpop Paris) to filter the feed to that circle only.
12. Tap "All" to return to the combined feed.
13. Scroll the feed to browse BeReal cards.
14. Tap "Follow" on any card to follow that user. The button toggles to "Following". Tap again to unfollow.
15. Tap the comment button to open the Comments screen for that post.
16. Tap the send button to open the Send To sheet.
17. Tap the RealMojis row (emoji faces + count) to open the RealMojis sheet.


COMMENTS SCREEN
---------------
18. The Comments screen slides up from the bottom.
19. View comments from other users in the circle.
20. Tap the ← back arrow (top left) to close and return to the feed.


REALMOJIS SHEET
---------------
21. The RealMojis sheet slides up from the bottom.
22. The main circle shows the selected user's face and their emoji reaction.
23. Tap any thumbnail at the bottom to switch to that user's RealMoji.
24. Tap anywhere outside the sheet (dark backdrop) to close.


SEND TO SHEET
-------------
25. The Send To sheet slides up from the bottom.
26. Shows friends you can send the BeReal to, plus external share options (Instagram, Snapchat, Messages, etc.).
27. Tap anywhere outside the sheet (dark backdrop) to close.


POST FLOW (CAMERA BUTTON)
--------------------------
28. Tap the camera button (⬤ bottom center) from any screen to open the Post screen.
29. Your BeReal preview is shown with a selfie overlay and retake count.
30. Tap "Audience" to cycle through sharing options: My Friends → Friends of Friends → Only Me.
31. Tap "Circles" to open the Circle Selector sheet — choose which Interest Circles to also share your BeReal to.
    - Only circles you selected during cold start appear here.
    - Tap a circle row to check/uncheck it.
    - Tap "Done" to confirm your selection.
32. Tap "Send" (top right) to post your BeReal. A confirmation toast appears.
33. Tap "Cancel" (top left) to discard and return to the feed.


PROFILE
-------
34. Tap the Profile icon (bottom nav, rightmost) to view your profile.
35. If you have selected Interest Circles, a "Your Circles" section appears with circle chips.
36. Tap any circle chip to jump directly to that circle's feed in Let's BeReal.
37. Tap the Settings gear icon (top right of profile cover) to open Settings.


SETTINGS
--------
38. The Settings screen shows a FEATURES section at the top.
39. Tap "Interest Circles" to open the Manage Circles screen.
    - Your active circles are listed with a Remove option.
    - Tap "Remove" next to any circle to remove it.
    - Tap "Add more circles" to open the Edit Circles screen (same as cold start, pre-filled with your current selections).
    - In Edit Circles, tap interests to add or remove them, then tap "Save" to apply changes.
40. Tap the ← back arrow to return from any sub-screen to the previous screen.
